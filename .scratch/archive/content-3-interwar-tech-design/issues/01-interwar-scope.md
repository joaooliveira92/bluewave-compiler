# CONTENT-3 — 01: INTERWAR slice scope

## Question

Which slice should CONTENT-3 deliver, and what is deferred? (Continues the
CONTENT-2 precedent.)

## Type

decision

## Status

resolved

## Notes

- Accepted owning capabilities: Research (B10b), Ship Design (B10b), Economy,
  Construction. Ship-design + research tech content in supported fields is
  authorable now with zero engine change.
- INTERWAR novel unit families are M4/M5: aircraft/carriers (M4), radar/electronics
  and missiles (M5), amphibians ($). Their unit mechanics are not accepted, so
  reachable research techs in those fields would violate DESIGN-55 §14.
- Deployment (DESIGN-25, unassigned) and blockades (B10c, blocked) remain deferred.

## Answer

**CONTENT-3 = INTERWAR-band (Jan 1920 – Dec 1939) technology + ship-design
continuation** of `content/production/1880/`, `SUPPORTED_MECHANICAL`, acyclic,
seeded on PRE_WAR tails (tickets 02–03). **Deferred (documented, not
fabricated):** AVIATION, CARRIER_OPERATIONS, RADAR_AND_ELECTRONICS, MISSILES,
AMPHIBIOUS_OPERATIONS INTERWAR techs (M4/M5/$), and deployment/blockades as before.

Sources: SPRINT-PLAN; AUTONOMOUS-AGENT stop conditions; DESIGN-55 §12/§14/§20.
