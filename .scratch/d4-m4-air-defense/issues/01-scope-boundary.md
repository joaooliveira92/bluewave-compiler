## Question

What vertical slice of DESIGN-45 belongs in D-4 vs deferred to E-track?

DESIGN-45 defines 8 defense layers: CAP, HEAVY_SAM, MEDIUM_SAM, LIGHT_SAM, HEAVY_AA, MEDIUM_AA, LIGHT_AA, CIWS_TERMINAL_DEFENSE.

**D-4 scope (in):** HEAVY_AA, MEDIUM_AA, LIGHT_AA, CIWS_TERMINAL_DEFENSE, director scaling, radar-directed medium-AA groups, disruption, ammunition, damage/power states, records, ordering, reason codes, contracts, engine stage wiring.

**E-track scope (deferred):** HEAVY_SAM, MEDIUM_SAM, LIGHT_SAM — these are missile systems requiring the M5 electronics/missile era mechanics.

**Decision:** Confirm HEAVY_AA + MEDIUM_AA + LIGHT_AA + CIWS as the D-4 vertical slice. SAM layers are explicitly deferred with documented DESIGN-55 §14 reachability markers.
