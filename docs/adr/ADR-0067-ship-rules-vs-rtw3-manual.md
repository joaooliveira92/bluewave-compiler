# ADR-0067: Ship classification and refit rules versus the RTW3 manual

## Status

Accepted 2026-10-08. Evidence: `docs/research/rtw3-official-manual-ship-rules-and-1890s-history.md` and `docs/research/rtw3-ship-design-guide-danger-honk.md`.

## Decision

1. **CA main-gun bound stays strictly above 6 in** (`docs/rules/ship-classification.md` §11, `classification.ts`). The RTW3 manual says "at least 6 in"; Bluewave deliberately gives a 6 in gun to `CL`. This is an intentional deviation, not a bug.
2. **No pre-1925 speed cap.** The 21 kn claim from a Steam comment has no support in the manual or elsewhere; `machinery.ts` keeps no date-based speed cap.
3. **`ARMOUR_MODIFICATION` stays broader than the manual's rebuild list** (manual p. 32: +1 in deck, remove conning tower, change upper belt). Bluewave allows the whole `ARMOUR_SCHEME` category under the REBUILD limits (life, fatigue, growth budget, cost), per spec §16. Turret-only armour removal from the community guide is not adopted. `RefitPlan` gained optional `armourSet` / `armourRemove` armour-region edits (ticket 21), legal only under `ARMOUR_MODIFICATION` and counted in net added mass, growth budget and cost through the normal compile path; the `ARMOUR_SCHEME` component category remains in `allowedCategories` but no content uses it. Armour availability (`ArmourBalance.materialAvailability`: required tech, introduction/obsolete month, maximum thickness) is checked by one shared gate, `armourAvailabilityFindings`, used by `compileDesign` for new designs and by `evaluateRefit` for `armourSet` (ticket 24); a material with no table entry is unrestricted, and the 1880 content has no entries yet (ticket 25).
4. **Community-guide values are not constants.** Hit-zone percentages (e.g. turret top), build-month figures and tech years from the guide are unverified balance values and are not copied.
5. **Deferred, not dropped:** turret-top hit region, main/secondary/tertiary battery distinction, all-or-nothing armour scheme (post-1900 abstractions). The protected-cruiser exception (ticket 19) and build-time-by-displacement (ticket 20) are implemented; the build-time table (ticket 22, `docs/research/ship-building-build-times.md`) now has no ESTIMATE rows, but the 390 t and 5,600 t rows are SECONDARY (displacement not taken from a primary source), so builds between 390 and 11,000 t are labelled provisional. The table uses typical (not record) durations: 18 months for destroyers per Brassey, with the efficient-industry multiplier covering records. Above 14,900 t it is non-monotonic (later battleships were built faster); an era multiplier is ticket 26.
6. **Per-yard capacity (ticket 23, supersedes the global `yardCapacityWorkPerMonth` placeholder).** `environment.facilities` now lists three yards (`yard_small` 3,000 t, `yard_medium` 9,000 t, `yard_large` 30,000 t, limits ESTIMATE) and one rate `yardWorkPerTonneMonth: 700` (ESTIMATE). Every line of a yard applies `floor(maxDisplacementKg x rate / 1000)` work per month (integer arithmetic; 2.1M, 6.3M and 21M), so a bigger yard works faster and the "limited by <line>" bottleneck is meaningful. The workshop forecast simulates the schedule in every yard whose length, beam, displacement and crane limits fit the hull and takes the yard with the smallest `max(historical, schedule)`, the smallest yard on a tie; the forecast therefore still equals `max(historical table time, capacity schedule)` and `forecast.yardId` names the yard. A Majestic-size hull (276M work) fits only `yard_large`, whose 21M keeps the ticket-20 behaviour (it finishes inside `maxMonths`). When a fitting yard exists but none finishes within `maxMonths` the forecast is null with a note naming the largest fitting yard; when no yard fits the hull the note is "No shipyard can build this hull." (the design already carries the BLOCKING `NO_FACILITY_FITS` finding). Campaign-owned yards and their real capacity stay with SB-14b.

## Consequences

Items 1-4 needed no change to existing rules; tickets 19 (protected-cruiser exception) and 21 (refit armour edits) added code. Build-time constants must be derived from history and verified against Conway's or Brassey before use (ticket 20).
