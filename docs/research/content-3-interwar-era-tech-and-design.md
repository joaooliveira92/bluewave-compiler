# Research: Content Sprint 3 — INTERWAR Era Naval Technology & Ship Design (1920–1939)

Historical research notes for scoping the INTERWAR-band (January 1920 – December
1939) technology + ship-design slice of the `scen_1880` production pack.
Research-only; cited per the "research, don't invent" rule. Grounding performed
by a research worker against high-trust sources (Wikipedia class/theory articles),
with four anchor classes verified by direct fetch (Nelson, King George V, North
Carolina, Yamato).

## Headline grounding

- **Washington Naval Treaty (1922):** capped battleship displacement at 35,000 t
  and gun calibre at 16-in; produced the first "treaty battleships" (Nelson,
  UK) and the 10,000 t / 8-in heavy-cruiser class. Source: _Washington Naval Treaty_; _Nelson-class battleship_.
- **16-in guns:** Nelson (BL 16-in Mk I, triple turrets, 1927) first RN 16-in;
  North Carolina (US 16-in/45, 1937). Source: _Nelson-class battleship_; _North Carolina-class battleship_.
- **14-in treaty guns:** King George V adopted BL 14-in Mk VII to hold the Second
  London Treaty calibre (chosen Oct 1935); quadruple turrets. Source: _King George V-class battleship (1939)_.
- **Fast battleships (1937):** North Carolina / King George V ~28 kt balancing
  speed/armour/firepower under 35,000 t; high-pressure 400 psi steam and geared
  turbines (121,000 shp). Sources: _North Carolina-class battleship_; _King George V-class battleship (1939)_.
- **Analog fire control:** Admiralty Fire Control Table (Mk I, ~1930s) and US Ford
  RangeKeeper solved gunnery solutions mechanically. Source: _Admiralty Fire Control Table_; _Ford Rangekeeper_.
- **Type 93 torpedo:** Japanese oxygen ("Long Lance") torpedo, ~1933-35,
  exceptional range. Source: _Type 93 torpedo_.
- **ASDIC/sonar:** British active sonar matured to a serviceable interwar
  submarine detector (~1928). Source: _ASDIC_.
- **Submarines:** Type VII-era diesel-electric and long-range fleet boats (Gato,
  RN T-class) by 1939. Sources: _German Type VII submarine_; _Gato-class submarine_.

## Thrusts (by field)

- GUNNERY: 16-in heavy guns (1927), super-heavy AP (1937), 14-in treaty (1937),
  quadruple turret (1936).
- MACHINERY: high-pressure steam (1937), improved geared turbines (1937).
- HULL_CONSTRUCTION: internal inclined belt (1927), void-liquid-void side
  protection (1937), improved cemented armour (1937), thickened belt/deck (1937).
- SHIP_DESIGN: Washington treaty design (1922), treaty cruiser design (1922),
  fast battleship design (1937).
- FIRE_CONTROL: analog fire-control computer (1932), gyro-stabilised director (1935).
- TORPEDOES: improved long-range (1933), oxygen torpedo (1933).
- MINES_AND_ASW: ASDIC/sonar (1928), improved moored contact mine (1930).
- SUBMARINES: modern diesel-electric (1935), fleet/ocean submarine (1939).
- INFRASTRUCTURE: fleet support basins & pens (1938).
- DAMAGE_CONTROL: enhanced subdivision/counterflooding (1937), magazine
  anti-flash protection (1936).
